ResuNova Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

ResuNova ("the app", "we") is a resume and cover letter builder made by Yahya Khan. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: your resumes are stored on your own device. Information leaves your device only when you choose to use AI writing help or Cloud Backup, or when you share a file yourself. We do not show ads, do not use analytics or tracking, and never sell your data.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you type into the app stays on your phone unless you use one of the optional online features below. This includes your name, contact details (email, phone, location, profile links), work history, education, skills, other resume sections, cover letters, an optional profile photo, and the job applications you track. You can edit or delete any of it in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device.

2. AI writing help (optional)

When you tap an AI button (for example "Improve with AI", "AI rewrite" or "Tailor to a job"), the app sends the text needed for that request, such as your summary, a bullet point, your job title, skills or a job description you pasted, to Google's Gemini models through Google Firebase AI Logic. The text is sent only when you tap the button. Your photo is never sent.

This feature uses Google's no-cost service tier. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use the content of these requests to provide and improve its products. Please don't include information in AI requests that you don't want to share with Google. AI suggestions can be wrong, so review them before you use them.

3. Cloud Backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google in Settings, the app copies your resumes (including your profile photo, when it fits) to a private storage space in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) that only your account can access. We receive your Google account's email address and a user ID from Firebase Authentication so the backup belongs to you. Your resumes stay on your device as well.

When you delete a resume while signed in, its cloud copy is deleted too. You can sign out at any time. To delete all of your backed-up data, contact us (see below) and we will delete it.

4. App security checks

To stop misuse of the online features, the app uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity. This lets Google confirm that requests come from the genuine ResuNova app on a real device. Google processes basic device and app information for this check.

5. Files you share

When you download, print or share a PDF, Word (.docx) or data (.json) file, the app creates the file on your device and opens your phone's share or print screen. Where the file goes after that (email, messaging, cloud storage) is your choice and follows that service's own policy.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera and photos: only if you choose to add a profile photo.
  • Internet: used only for AI writing help, Cloud Backup and the security checks above.

7. What we don't do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising IDs or tracking.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not share your information with anyone other than Google, which provides the AI, Cloud Backup and security services described above on our behalf.

8. Service providers

The online features are provided by Google. Their handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information.

9. Security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Cloud Backup data can only be read by your own signed-in account. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, but we use these standard protections.

10. Children

ResuNova is intended for people aged 16 and over who are preparing job applications. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the online features, contact us and we will delete the related data.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app fully offline by not using AI writing help or Cloud Backup.
  • Turn off AI writing help in Settings.
  • Edit, export or delete your resumes in the app at any time.
  • Ask us to delete your Cloud Backup data by contacting us.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
